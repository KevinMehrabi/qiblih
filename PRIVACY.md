# Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 8, 2026

Qiblih Compass is a simple compass utility for finding the direction of the Baha'i Qiblih.

## Data Collection

Qiblih Compass does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

## Location

Qiblih Compass requests access to your location while the app is in use so it can calculate the direction from your current position to the Qiblih.

Your location is processed on your device only. The app does not store your location and does not send your location to any server.

## Compass Heading

Qiblih Compass uses your device compass heading to show whether you should turn left, turn right, or are facing the Qiblih.

Compass heading data is processed on your device only and is not stored or transmitted.

## Networking, Accounts, Ads, and Tracking

Qiblih Compass does not require an account, does not use networking, does not show ads, does not use analytics, and does not track users.

## Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's privacy practices change.

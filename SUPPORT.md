## Troubleshooting

Qiblih Compass uses your iPhone's location and compass heading to calculate the direction to the Baha'i Qiblih.

If the compass appears inaccurate:

- Make sure Location Services are enabled for Qiblih Compass.
- Test on a physical iPhone with compass support.
- Move the phone in a figure-eight motion to calibrate the compass.
- Keep the phone away from magnets, metal surfaces, chargers, and electronics that may interfere with the compass sensor.

Qiblih Compass does not require an account and does not transmit or store your location.
